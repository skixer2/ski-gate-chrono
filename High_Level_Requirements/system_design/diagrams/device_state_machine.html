SGC device state machine — firmware 0.23.x (sleep era)

LED colors are the athlete-facing language: the diagram teaches what each color means. Wire states: SLEEP reports in the old IDLE slot (RC 0).

FW 0.22.x and earlier: no SLEEP/SYSTEM OFF — the device stayed always-on (LED toggled blue 1 s on / 1 s off while advertising). The 100 ms/2 s heartbeat arrives with 0.23.1.
